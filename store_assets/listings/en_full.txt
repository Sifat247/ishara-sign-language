Ishara is a free, offline beginner's guide to Bangla Sign Language (BdSL) and American Sign Language (ASL). Use it to learn greetings, everyday phrases, emergency and health words, family terms and numbers, with short text hints, quizzes and a searchable guide.

WHAT YOU GET
• 28 beginner signs in 4 topics (plus 3 ASL letters): Greetings & daily needs, Emergency & healthcare, Family and Numbers
• Pick a track: BdSL, ASL or both
• Interactive quiz drills with instant feedback, XP and progress saved on your device
• Searchable sign guide with Bangla and English words, plus a bookmarks list
• Left-hand / right-hand flip view
• Interface in 7 languages: Bangla, English, Arabic, Hindi, Urdu, Spanish and French
• 100% offline: no internet, no account, no permissions needed
• No ads, no in-app purchases, no data collection

IMPORTANT: PLEASE READ
Ishara is a beginner's study aid. Signs are shown as an emoji with a written description of the hand movement. It does not contain videos, and it is not an official, certified or complete sign language reference, nor is it endorsed by any deaf association or government body. Sign language varies by region and community. To learn properly, practise with deaf teachers or a recognised institute.
